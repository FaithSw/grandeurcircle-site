The problem

Fragmented.
Reactive.
Unclear.

Arriving in the UK means navigating a system that was never designed to be navigated. Everything exists, but nothing connects.

Most people piece it together through Facebook groups, Reddit threads, and word of mouth. Some get it right. Many don't.

  • Housing - Right to Rent, deposits, area knowledge
  • Work - visa rules, hour limits, compliant listings
  • Healthcare - NHS registration, GP access by postcode
  • Tax - NI numbers, HMRC, PAYE from day one
  • Community - scam protection, local integration

"The information exists. It's just scattered across 12 different websites, 3 Facebook groups, and a WhatsApp chat from 2019."

This is what we heard across 100+ interviews with international students and skilled workers at UK universities. The problem isn't access to information. It's structure.

Grandeur Circle brings structure to this.

One platform. Postcode-aware. Arrival-stage sequenced. Visa-compliant.
